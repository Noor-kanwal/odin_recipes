Lasagna

What distinguishes chicken karahi from other curries from the region is that traditionally, it is cooked without onions in the base, and instead uses just tomatoes, ginger and garlic. It is usually served with naan, roti or rice. This dish is common in North Indian and Pakistani cuisine.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 (32 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce
  • 32 ounces cottage cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 2 Eggs
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 teaspoons dried parsley
  • salt to taste
  • ground black pepper to taste
  • 9 Lasagna noodles
  • ½ cup water

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. In a large skillet over medium heat, cook and stir ground beef until browned and crumbly, about 5 minutes. Drain and discard grease. Stir spaghetti sauce into the skillet with the beef; heat through.
  3. In a mixing bowl, combine cottage cheese, 2 cups mozzarella cheese, eggs, Parmesan cheese, parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  4. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles in the boiling water, stirring occasionally until tender yet firm to the bite, about 8 minutes. Drain.
  5. Spread 1 cup of the meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Layer with three noodles, one third of the cottage cheese mixture, and one third of the remaining meat sauce. Repeat layers twice more, ending with meat sauce. Pour water around the edges of the dish.
  6. Cover with aluminum foil: to prevent sticking, either spray foil with cooking spray, or make sure the foil does not touch the cheese.
  7. Bake in preheated oven until bubbly, about 45 minutes. Remove foil; sprinkle with remaining 1 cup mozzarella cheese. Continue baking uncovered until cheese is melted, about 15 minutes more. Let stand 15 minutes before serving.
